Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up or attending a Madrasa on Suffah.

Why should we switch from free WhatsApp / Zoom to Suffah?

While WhatsApp and Zoom appear “free”, they actually cost madaris thousands in lost tuition fees, wasted teaching time, and severe modesty risks. On WhatsApp, ustazas lose track of daily Sabaq/Manzil, audio clips get lost, and ~20-30% of fees are never collected. Free Zoom cuts off abruptly every 40 minutes and carries the constant risk of accidental video/camera exposure for banat. Suffah provides a zero-camera purdah sanctuary, synchronized Mushaf pages, automated daily registers, and direct institutional UPI payment routing that recovers your lost fees in month 1.

How does the 100% Free Forever “Barakah Free” Plan work?

We believe in Khidmat-e-Deen. Any small village maktab, independent ustaza, or home Quran academy with up to 25 students can use Suffah completely free forever with zero expiration date and no credit card required. As your student enrollment grows, you can easily upgrade to the Maktab or Jamiya plan with one tap.

Does Suffah take any percentage or commission from our student fees?

0% Platform Commission. When parents pay student fees via UPI, 100% of the funds transfer directly from the parent’s UPI app straight into your Madrasa’s official bank account. Suffah never touches, holds, or deducts fees from your institution’s money.

How fast can our Madrasa get started?

In less than 5 minutes! Simply click “Register Madrasa” on this website or in the Android app. Our setup team will configure your custom digital campus, issue your Mudir credentials, and help you add your teachers and students immediately.

Can any Madrasa in the world register on Suffah?

Yes! Any Islamic seminary, Darul Uloom, local maktab, or private online Quran academy across India, the UK, USA, UAE, Saudi Arabia, Pakistan, South Africa, or elsewhere can register directly through the app by clicking “Register Madrasa”. Each gets an isolated tenant environment with custom branding.

How does direct institutional UPI fee collection work?

When a Madrasa owner configures their institutional UPI VPA (e.g. yourmadrasa@upi), student fee payments and donor contributions generate dynamic UPI deep-links that route 100% of the funds straight into the seminary's bank account with 0% platform intermediary commission.

Can students or teachers of one Madrasa see data from another?

No. Suffah enforces bank-grade tenant data isolation through Google Cloud Firestore security rules. A Mudir, teacher, or student can only see records, batches, and chat channels belonging to their own registered Madrasa.

How does Purdah protection work in live classes?

Suffah disables video camera streaming entirely at the code and permission level. Classes are crystal-clear, audio-first sessions. Teachers can share digital Mushaf pages and Islamic texts, while students take turns reciting under complete modesty.

How are authentic Sanad certificates verified?

Every certificate issued through Suffah carries a unique serial number and cryptographic verification link. Anyone can enter the serial number on our public verification portal (verify-sanad.html) to verify authenticity, issuing madrasa, and grades.

How can I install the app on Android?

You can download the production APK directly from this website (suffah-release-v50.apk) or install from the Google Play Store under package com.madrasatussalikat.lilbanat.
