Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 31, 2026

Madarsa Tus Salikat Lil Banat provides Islamic learning services through mobile apps, live classes, chat, assignments, payments, donations, notifications, and admin support tools.

Data We Collect

  • Profile and authentication data: name, email address, role, account approval status, email verification status, avatar/profile photo, referral details, Firebase identifiers, and login metadata.
  • Learning data: course enrollments, progress, assignments, submissions, teacher feedback, quiz/class activity, and attendance records.
  • Chat, status, and media: messages, read receipts, comments, reactions, status posts, images, videos, audio, documents, file names, file sizes, and moderation evidence.
  • Live classes: class metadata, participant lists, Agora channel/session identifiers, microphone/camera state, reconnect details, attendance duration, and recording metadata when recording is enabled.
  • Payments and donations: payment type, amount, submitted reference numbers, review status, admin notes, reconciliation/audit data, and entitlement/enrollment updates. Do not submit card numbers or bank passwords in app text fields.
  • Notifications: Expo/Firebase push tokens, notification preferences, delivery metadata, and reminder history.
  • Location: the app is not designed to collect precise background location. If a future feature requests location, it should be disclosed in the app permission prompt and Play Store listing.
  • Security and analytics: device/app diagnostics, operational logs, security events, duplicate-upload checks, abuse-prevention events, and performance/error telemetry.

How We Use Data

We use data to create and approve accounts, deliver courses, run live classes, manage assignments, send notifications, verify manual payments and donations, provide support, protect students, moderate content, prevent fraud/abuse, and comply with legal, safety, accounting, or dispute obligations.

Third-Party Processors

We use trusted service providers to operate the app, including Firebase for authentication, Firestore, storage, and messaging; Railway/FastAPI backend hosting; Agora for live audio/video and recording services; Razorpay/payment references where applicable; Expo/push notification services; and related storage or infrastructure providers.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only as needed for app operations, support, legal compliance, safety review, payment/donation reconciliation, or with authorized madrasa administrators and teachers according to their roles.

Retention

We keep data while your account is active or while needed for education, safety, legal, audit, payment, dispute, or accounting purposes. Some records may be deleted, anonymized, or retained where required by law or safety obligations.

Account Deletion and User Rights

You may request account deletion or data export in the app through Settings → Data & Privacy, or by using our public account deletion form: Account Deletion Request.

Children and Student Safety

This is an education platform. Students should use the app only with appropriate family/guardian and madrasa supervision where required. We use moderation and admin review tools to support a safe learning environment.
